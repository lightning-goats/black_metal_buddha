Wear the image. Remember the teaching.

Black Metal Buddha is an independent apparel project exploring impermanence, mortality, non-self, and contemplative symbolism through an extreme-metal visual language.

These designs are not a substitute for Buddhist practice or lineage. They are visual reminders: forms decay, identities shift, and nothing held tightly stays unchanged.
