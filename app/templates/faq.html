FAQ

Questions before the first drop.

Can I buy the shirts yet?

Not yet. The storefront is in Phase 0 while physical samples are being approved. Checkout will open only after production and fulfillment are validated.

Who will print and ship the shirts?

The current plan is automated print-on-demand fulfillment through Printful. Exact product details will be published before sales open.

How will payments work?

Phase 1 will use Square for fiat checkout. Lightning payments are intentionally deferred until Square offers an official API path that can settle Lightning payments automatically to fiat for this ecommerce flow.

Are the designs connected to a Buddhist organization?

No. Black Metal Buddha is an independent art and apparel project and does not represent a Buddhist lineage, temple, teacher, or institution.
