Hi — this package contains four Black Metal Buddha designs intended for
sample printing on a black T-shirt.

Please review START_HERE_FOR_PRINTER.txt and PRINT_SPECIFICATIONS.json before
printing.

PRODUCTION FILES
All production canvases are 12 x 16 inches, 3600 x 4800 pixels, transparent,
and nominally 300 pixels/inch.

For Lotus of the Void, Dharma of Decay, and Meditate on Death:
- 01_transparent_png contains the closest raster version to the approved art.
- 02_two_ink_vector and 03_two_ink_png contain simplified two-ink alternatives.
- These three originated from generated shirt mockups; please inspect their
  effective detail carefully at the intended output size.

For Longchenpa — Rest in Illusion:
- 02_two_ink_vector/longchenpa_rest_in_illusion_two_ink.svg is the canonical
  production master.
- 01_transparent_png/longchenpa_rest_in_illusion_12x16_300dpi.png and
  03_two_ink_png/longchenpa_rest_in_illusion_two_ink_12x16_300dpi.png are
  matching transparent 3600 x 4800 raster exports derived from that SVG.
- The intended inks are Bone #E9E1D5 and Ritual Red #971F1F, with the black
  garment supplying negative space.

Please choose the appropriate source for the selected process; do not stack
the 01 and 03 versions on top of one another.

Do not print files from 04_previews or 05_original_mockups. Those are visual
reference proofs only.

Please preserve transparent knockout areas and intentional distressed texture.
Confirm:
- permitted print dimensions on the chosen garment
- top-of-print / chest placement
- handling of the dark-garment underbase
- minimum printable line and knockout sizes
- red reproduction
- whether any process-specific simplification is required

Please provide a proof and one physical sample for approval before any broader
production run.

Thank you.
