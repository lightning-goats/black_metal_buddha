BLACK METAL BUDDHIST — PRINTER HANDOFF
Prepared September 17, 2026

STATUS: READY TO SEND FOR PRINTER REVIEW AND A SAMPLE, NOT PRESS-APPROVED

The source images were generated shirt mockups, not flat high-resolution
production masters. The artwork has been isolated from those mockups. This
package preserves the existing composition and lettering; it does not claim
a full-resolution redraw. Do not print the shirt photographs themselves.

CANVAS, SIZE AND GARMENT
All production canvases are 12 x 16 inches (30.48 x 40.64 cm).
The artwork is proportionally fitted and centered, with approximately
0.25 inches of transparent margin above and below. Use a BLACK garment.
The black-looking interior details and distress holes are transparent.
Do not substitute a white or pale garment without adapting the art.

  Lotus of the Void: approx. 10.45 x 15.50 inches of artwork.
    Extracted source: 664 x 985 pixels.
    Original raster detail at supplied size: approx. 64 ppi.
  Dharma of Decay: approx. 11.17 x 15.50 inches of artwork.
    Extracted source: 702 x 974 pixels.
    Original raster detail at supplied size: approx. 63 ppi.
  Meditate on Death: approx. 11.03 x 15.50 inches of artwork.
    Extracted source: 681 x 957 pixels.
    Original raster detail at supplied size: approx. 62 ppi.

The printer must confirm the allowed print area for the actual shirt model,
size and process. Scale proportionally; do not stretch to fill the canvas.
Placement on the garment is not baked in: agree on chest placement before
printing. A preview may trim transparent padding automatically, so confirm
the actual artwork dimensions rather than relying only on upload settings.

IMPORTANT RESOLUTION DISCLOSURE
The original mockups are 1254 x 1254 pixels, but the design occupies only
part of each image. The continuous-tone PNGs are enlarged from the extracted
artwork; setting a 300-ppi export does NOT create native 300-ppi detail.
Vector tracing gives scalable edges, but also does not recover lost detail.
These files are useful for printer assessment and a physical sample. They
should not be treated as automatically approved for a large production run.

REQUIRED BEFORE A RUN
Inspect all lettering, thin ornamental strokes, small gaps and grain at the
actual output size. Compare both file variants with their matching previews.
The many small distressed marks are intentional, but the shop may need to
simplify tiny islands, enlarge minimum lines/gaps, or adjust the artwork for
its equipment. Obtain approval before making material visual changes.
The printer must determine underbase, color matching, trapping/choke, ink
sequence, mesh/halftone settings and final separations as applicable. The
files do not contain a pre-engineered underbase or registration marks.
Some edge pixels in the raster PNGs are anti-aliased. Review how the chosen
process handles them on black fabric. Request one physical sample and approve
its fine detail, color, hand-feel and placement before ordering a larger run.

REFERENCE
Printful, "How to prepare perfect print files for print-on-demand"
(updated July 28, 2025; consulted September 17, 2026) discusses PNG/sRGB,
product-specific print areas, source resolution and dark-shirt transparency.
The shop's specifications for the selected product take precedence.
