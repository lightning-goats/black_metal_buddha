This package contains four Black Metal Buddha designs. All production canvases
are 12 x 16 inches (3600 x 4800 pixels) at nominal 300 pixels/inch and are
intended for a BLACK garment.

IMPORTANT SOURCE DIFFERENCE
The first three designs — Lotus of the Void, Dharma of Decay, and Meditate on
Death — originated as generated shirt mockups. Their transparent raster files
were isolated and enlarged from those mockups, and their SVGs are automatic
two-ink traces. They are suitable for printer review and sample validation but
do not contain native 300-ppi source detail.

Longchenpa — Rest in Illusion is different. Its canonical production source is
the approved flat two-ink SVG master:
  02_two_ink_vector/longchenpa_rest_in_illusion_two_ink.svg

Its 3600 x 4800 transparent PNGs are deterministically rendered from that
vector master. They are not extracted from a shirt photograph.

TRANSPARENCY AND BLACK GARMENT

The black-looking negative areas inside the designs are transparent so the
black garment supplies those values. Do not flatten the production files onto
a black rectangle. Do not substitute a white or pale garment without adapting
the artwork.

The Longchenpa PNGs contain transparent, bone-white, ritual-red and
anti-aliased edge pixels produced by vector rasterization. Its color intent is
the two named inks above; process-specific underbase and edge handling are the
printer's responsibility.

FIRST THREE DESIGNS — RESOLUTION DISCLOSURE

For Lotus of the Void, Dharma of Decay, and Meditate on Death, a 300-ppi file
header does not create native 300-ppi source detail. Their source mockups were
1254 x 1254 pixels and the artwork occupied only part of each source image.
Vector tracing improves scalability of traced edges but cannot restore detail
that was absent from the original raster.

Longchenpa is not subject to that specific limitation because its production
raster is rendered from the approved vector master.

REQUIRED BEFORE A PRODUCTION RUN

For every design:
- inspect lettering at actual output size
- inspect thin ornamental strokes and tiny knockout gaps
- preserve intentional distressed texture
- confirm crop and chest placement
- confirm white / bone coverage on black fabric
- confirm ritual-red reproduction
- verify any required underbase, choke/trap and process separations
- approve one physical sample before a broader run

For Printful specifically, use the currently selected product's live print
template and current file requirements as authoritative for placement and
maximum print area.
